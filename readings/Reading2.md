
At the start of the interview, Schön distinguishes between knowing in action and reflection in action. Knowing in action refers to behaviors or skills that we have become so familiar with that we no longer need to consciously think about them, such as walking or breathing. Reflection in action, on the other hand, is essential to the design process. A good designer needs to consciously reflect on what they are doing while they are doing it, constantly observing, questioning, and adjusting their decisions. This is necessary because, as Schön states, “There is no direct path between the designer's intention and the outcome.” The design process is always full of unexpected results, so a designer cannot simply follow one path to the end or “fall in love with an initial design idea.” Instead, the process often becomes a cycle: make → encounter something unexpected → reflect on why it happened → adjust → make again. 
In my opinion, coming up with an initial idea can sometimes be similar to knowing in action. An experienced designer might naturally sketch, compose, or make decisions using knowledge that has become almost instinctive through years of practice. This kind of fluency can certainly be impressive, but an initial design intention can never be assumed to be perfect. Even if the original idea turns out to work extremely well, the designer still needs to arrive at that conclusion through continuous experimentation and reflection rather than simply assuming that the first idea was correct.
Speaking of my own experience, for this Pong exercise, I was too into my first idea, and it was very hard for me to come up with any improvements or adjustments. I believe I was not executing the reflection in action very well.  To solve my dilemma, I might use another thinking method Schön mentioned, provided by another designer Hannah Arendt, named stop and think. The designer stops the design process, pauses and reflects on what job has been done, and continues with a new goal.  I reflect on my Pong project, and concluded that I mostly changed the art of the game and did not do enough modification of the game itself.
